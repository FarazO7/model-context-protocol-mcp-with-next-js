# Statement of Work: Store Finder and Loyalty App, Phase 1

FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR A PRODUCT DEMO. All companies and people are invented.

- Supplier: Brightfield Digital Pvt Ltd, a Udyam-registered micro enterprise
- Client: Acme Retail Pvt Ltd
- Reference: BFD-ACME-SOW-07, dated 1 September 2026
- Client approver: Meera Iyer, Head of Digital, meera.iyer@acme-retail.example

## 1. Scope

Design and build an iOS and Android app for Acme's 140 stores, covering a store finder and a loyalty wallet.

## 2. Milestones and fees (₹, excluding GST)

### M1. Discovery and UX design: ₹2,40,000

Deliverables: research summary, clickable prototype, design system.

Acceptance criteria:
- (a) The prototype covers store search, store detail, loyalty card and points history.
- (b) The design system documents colours, typography and core components.
- (c) Acme's product owner has attended at least one review session.

### M2. Store finder (iOS and Android): ₹4,80,000

Acceptance criteria:
- (a) Users can search stores by pin code and by current location.
- (b) Results show distance, opening hours and an in-stock badge.
- (c) The store detail screen hands off directions to Google Maps (Android) and Apple Maps (iOS).
- (d) Crash-free sessions of at least 99.5% over three days of staging tests.
- (e) Passes Acme's accessibility checklist v2 (contrast and font scaling).

### M3. Loyalty wallet: ₹3,60,000

Acceptance criteria:
- (a) Users can register with a mobile number and OTP.
- (b) A digital loyalty card with a barcode that scans at Acme tills.
- (c) Points balance and history sync from Acme's loyalty API within 5 minutes.
- (d) A push notification when points are credited.

### M4. Launch and 30-day hypercare: ₹1,20,000

Acceptance criteria:
- (a) Apps live on the App Store and Google Play.
- (b) Critical bugs fixed within 24 hours during hypercare.
- (c) Handover documentation and source code delivered.

Total: ₹12,00,000 plus GST.

## 3. Acceptance

The Supplier will send a delivery notice with an acceptance pack for each milestone. The Client will either accept in writing or raise a written objection listing the unmet criteria within 15 days of delivery. If no written objection is raised within 15 days, the milestone is deemed accepted.

## 4. Payment

The Supplier invoices on acceptance. The Client pays within 30 days of acceptance. As the Supplier is a micro enterprise, the MSMED Act's payment timelines apply where they are stricter.

## 5. Dependencies

Acme provides access to its store, inventory and loyalty APIs. Where the Supplier has delivered its side, delays or gaps in Acme's APIs do not count against the Supplier's acceptance criteria.
